# Negotiation Workflow

## Purpose
The chatbot should help customers and owners/agents move toward a fair agreement.

## Supported Actions
- collect customer offer
- send offer professionally
- request best available price from owner/agent
- update customer with negotiation results
- continue negotiation after customer approval

## MVP Recommendation
Use a **hybrid model**:
- rule-based boundaries for safe actions
- AI-assisted messaging for natural phrasing and negotiation flow

## Approval Logic
The system should define:
- what can be automated
- what needs human approval
- what must be logged
