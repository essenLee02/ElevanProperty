# Module: Home

## Purpose
The Home page introduces the company and explains the business clearly to visitors.

## Required Content
- company background
- company story
- vision and mission
- value proposition
- types of services provided
- customer benefits
- how the platform helps customers buy or rent properties

## UX Recommendations
- clear hero section with CTA
- short explanation of key services
- featured properties or featured locations
- clear call-to-action buttons:
  - Start Chat
  - Browse Properties
  - Contact Us

## Best Practice Additions
- Add trust indicators such as years of experience, completed transactions, or client testimonials
- Add SEO-friendly headings for city and property categories
- Add a persistent floating chatbot on the page
