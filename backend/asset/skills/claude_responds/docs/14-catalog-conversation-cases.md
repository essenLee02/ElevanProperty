# 14 - Catalog Cases
The `KATALOG NYATA AGENT INI` block is the only truth about availability, including what is empty.
Once the 4 slots are known, **show as soon as you can** - default **2** listings, or the number asked.
**Keep interviewing after the 4 slots are known** is the defect this prevents.
**Then it is natural to ask** one follow-up after the listings, when their reaction opens it.
